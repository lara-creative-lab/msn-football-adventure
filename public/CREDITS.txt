Music
-----
"世界杯荣耀曲" was composed procedurally for this game as an original
stadium-football instrumental. It is bundled as world-cup-stadium.wav and is
the default game music.

The former CC0 track remains bundled as adventure-theme.ogg for archive
compatibility but is no longer selected by the game.

Character Art
-------------
The ten stylized collectible football figurine assets were generated specifically
for this game with OpenAI image generation, then locally background-removed and optimized.
